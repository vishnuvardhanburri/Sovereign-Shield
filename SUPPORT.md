# Support Policy & Channels

Welcome to the Sovereign Shield support portal. As an enterprise-grade open-source security platform, we provide multiple support channels tailored for community users, security researchers, and enterprise deployments.

---

## Support Matrix

| Category | Channel | Response Time SLA | Target Audience |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Security Reports** | `security@sovereignshield.org` | 24 Hours | Security Researchers, CISOs |
| **Bug Reports** | [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/vishnuvardhanburri/Sovereign-Shield/issues) | 48 Hours | Community Developers |
| **Architecture Q&A** | [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/vishnuvardhanburri/Sovereign-Shield/discussions) | 3 Business Days | Open Community |
| **Enterprise SLA** | `support@xaviratech.com` | 1 Hour (24/7) | Paid Enterprise Buyers |

---

## Supported Releases

We maintain active security and bug fix support for the latest release on the `main` branch.

- **Current Version**: `v2.1.0` (Supported for security & bug fixes)
- **Previous Versions**: Support is deprecated 6 months after a major release. Upgrade to `v2.1.0+` is strongly recommended.

---

## Reporting Vulnerabilities

Do **NOT** post security vulnerabilities on public GitHub issues. Follow our [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) for private coordinated disclosure.
