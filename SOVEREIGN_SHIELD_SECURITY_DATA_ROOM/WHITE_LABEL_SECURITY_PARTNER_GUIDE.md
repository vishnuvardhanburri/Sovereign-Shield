# White-Label Security Partner Guide

## Partner Positioning

Sovereign Shield can be positioned by a partner as a human-governed security command center for private AI governance, evidence generation, compliance visibility, and operator-controlled security review.

Recommended partner phrase:

"A governance-first security platform that keeps humans accountable while accelerating AI risk analysis, detection, prioritization, and evidence reporting."

## Suitable Partners

- Managed security service providers
- Compliance advisory firms
- AI governance consultancies
- Enterprise security resellers
- Private-cloud platform integrators
- Regional data-residency specialists

## Partner Demo Flow

Use the standard five-minute demo:

1. Login
2. Security dashboard
3. Shadow AI detection
4. Governance controls
5. Audit chain
6. Evidence generation
7. Compliance visibility
8. Executive reporting

Partner-specific close:

"As your security partner, we operate the deployment and review workflow with your CISO-approved policies, while your organization retains accountability for critical decisions, compliance obligations, and risk acceptance."

## White-Label Boundaries

| Area | Partner Can Own | Customer Must Own |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Deployment | Install, configure, maintain | Approve infrastructure and data boundaries |
| Monitoring | Operate dashboards and alerts | Approve incident response policy |
| Evidence | Generate and package reports | Approve external use of evidence |
| Policy | Recommend rules and defaults | Approve final policy and exceptions |
| Quarantine review | Queue and document recommendations | Approve high-impact actions |
| Compliance | Provide technical evidence | Own legal interpretation and certification |

## Partner Delivery Package

- Branded demo script
- Customer-specific security architecture
- Governance workflow map
- Compliance evidence pack
- Threat model review
- Production readiness checklist
- Disaster recovery plan
- Monthly executive report template
- Quarterly control review package

## Partner Qualification Questions

- Does the customer use AI with regulated or confidential data?
- Does the customer need local inference or data residency?
- Who owns CISO approval?
- Who owns policy exceptions?
- What evidence must procurement see?
- What SIEM or ticketing system receives alerts?
- What backup and retention policies apply?
- What support SLA is required?

## Partner Risks

- Do not claim legal compliance certification.
- Do not claim customer critical actions happen without approval.
- Do not use synthetic demo metrics as production usage proof.
- Do not operate customer policy without written approval.
- Do not mix tenant evidence across customers.
- Do not store customer secrets outside approved secret management.

## Partner Success Metrics

- Time to controlled pilot
- Number of governed AI workflows
- Sensitive-data findings reduced before inference
- Review queue completion rate
- Evidence reports delivered on schedule
- Audit ledger integrity checks passed
- Backup and restore drills completed
- CISO sign-off obtained
