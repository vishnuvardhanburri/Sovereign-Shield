# CISO FAQ

## Why should a CISO care?

Sovereign Shield gives security leadership a governance-first control plane for private AI adoption. It makes sensitive-data handling, prompt security, route decisions, risk scoring, and evidence generation visible to accountable humans.

## What are the highest-value controls?

- PII pseudonymization before inference
- Prompt-injection detection
- Semantic DLP
- Local-first high-sensitivity routing
- Operator quarantine review
- Audit ledger
- Evidence report generation
- Enterprise command center visibility

## How are humans kept in control?

Critical outcomes are designed for review, approval, and audit. The system can identify, score, explain, and queue risk, but security leadership defines enforcement policy and owns final approval for critical actions.

## What evidence can I show the board?

- Executive security summary
- Control mapping
- Risk heatmap
- Evidence report
- Audit ledger verification
- Deployment readiness certificate
- Backup and restore drill result
- Known limitations and residual-risk register

## What is the biggest residual risk?

The biggest residual risk is operational ownership. The buyer must correctly configure secrets, infrastructure, monitoring, storage immutability, retention, identity governance, and response processes.

## How does local AI governance work?

High-sensitivity requests are routed to local Ollama-backed inference by policy. This reduces exposure to external providers while preserving supervised analysis workflows.

## What should be reviewed before production?

1. Secret management
2. RBAC mapping
3. Approval workflow
4. Ledger storage
5. Backup and restore
6. SIEM and alert routing
7. Local model performance
8. Compliance mappings
9. Incident-response process
10. Operational runbooks

## What should not be claimed?

Do not claim that the product removes human accountability, certifies legal compliance, or independently manages enterprise security posture. The correct claim is evidence-driven, human-governed security operations.
